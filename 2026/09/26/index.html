★★★ GRB 260924A may be among the most energetic GRBs ever observed

A new analysis combines the preliminary break-based redshift z=3.51, Fermi-GBM fluence, and Band-spectrum fit to infer

Eγ,iso≃9.74×1054 erg

in the rest-frame 1−104 keV band. This places GRB 260924A in the extreme tail of the GRB energy distribution and close to GRB 221009A—the “BOAT”—for which Konus-Wind inferred Eiso≃1.2×1055 erg. (New energetics estimate, GRB 221009A comparison)

The estimate uses the previously reported Fermi parameters

Epeak=645±4 keV,S10−1000keV=2.84×10−4 ergcm−2,

with a bolometric k-correction.

Status and caveats: Eiso is not the physical jet energy: no jet opening angle or beaming correction is yet available. The value also scales with the luminosity distance, while the present z=3.51±0.10 estimate is based mainly on Lyman breaks rather than secure high-significance metal lines. Continued optical spectroscopy and broadband afterglow monitoring are therefore essential before ranking it definitively against GRB 221009A.
