Wednesday's deadline normally appear at 20:00 Eastern on Thursday, which is
00:00 UTC on Friday. The website uses the UTC listing date, consistent with its
Monday–Friday report headings. See the [arXiv announcement schedule](https://info.arxiv.org/help/availability.html#announcement-schedule).
