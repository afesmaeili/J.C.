How the journal is prepared

Each daily report opens with the number of new primary submissions in the dated arXiv listings for astro-ph, hep-ph, hep-ex, hep-th, gr-qc, nucl-ex and nucl-th. Cross-lists and replacements are excluded from those totals; selected-paper counts elsewhere describe the editorial selection. The astro-ph total includes all six subcategories, with HE and CO shown separately when available.

Listing dates follow arXiv's UTC convention and differ from submission, event and publication dates. Papers are deduplicated across categories and earlier reports. Selected studies discuss methods, results, limitations and earlier literature under History; measurements, limits, forecasts and models are distinguished.

Alert reports group follow-ups and corrections by event, and distinguish candidates from confirmed counterparts. Science News covers non-alert field developments and research across the sciences using primary research or authoritative announcements. No new batch means no new-paper claim; significant weekend developments enter Monday's news.

Edition-specific source-check cutoffs, historical coverage notes, dated listing sources and retrieval limits remain in each downloadable Markdown file's metadata. A short section does not prove that every source was quiet, and screening a listing is distinct from reading every paper in full. Editorial dates and revision records are retained in the source metadata.
