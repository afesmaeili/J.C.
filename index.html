A daily guide to astroparticle physics literature

This daily journal club brings together interesting arXiv papers in astroparticle physics and noteworthy scientific news.

Explore the daily reports for concise summaries, context from the literature, and ideas for further reading and discussion.

What you will find

01

Selected research

ArXiv highlights include paper links, authors, concise explanations, and important equations or measurements. Shorter notes cover additional papers when relevant.

02

History

Literature comparisons explain what earlier studies established, what a new paper adds or challenges, and which uncertainties remain. References connect the discussion to the original work.

03

Verified science news

Updates cover experiments, observatories, discoveries, and mission milestones, with source links and event dates. Preliminary findings and unconfirmed associations are identified as such.

Reading the reports

Reports are organized by date. The opening statistics distinguish the selected papers from the new submissions in the full arXiv listings. Star ratings highlight reading priority; the summaries retain the scientific caveats.

On days without new arXiv announcements, an entry may contain science news only.

Explore the collection

Search papers by title, author, arXiv ID, topic, or words in the reading notes. Use the archive to browse earlier dates.

Each report can be downloaded as Markdown or printed to PDF. The HTML archive provides an offline copy of the collection.
